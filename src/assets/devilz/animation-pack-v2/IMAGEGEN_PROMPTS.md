# ImageGen prompts used for animation pack v2

Mode: built-in ImageGen. Accepted outputs only; the rejected first Devi draft
is intentionally omitted.

## Main sprite — Devi

Use case: sketch-to-render. Asset type: mobile-game character sprite, main
grounded pose. Image 1 is the only authority for character, silhouette,
anatomy, expression and pose. Image 2 is only the colored-pencil material
reference and must not supply anatomy. Preserve the very wide swept horns,
small semicircular head, side arms, X left eye, curved right eye, enormous
horizontal mouth, two fangs and central diamond mark. Keep the front-facing
child-drawn asymmetry and black line. Fill with deep coral/red-orange pencil
and wax crayon, salmon highlights, burgundy cross-hatched shadows and small
chartreuse accents. One centered full character, square canvas, generous
padding. Perfectly flat `#00FFFF` chroma backdrop; no shadow, gradient, floor,
halo, text, watermark, rectangular fills, new anatomy or Carlitos features.

## Pose sheets — common structure

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production animation pose sheet for a
mobile Phaser runner. The supplied finished main sprite is authoritative for
identity, palette, tactile child-drawn ink, orientation and anatomy. Produce
one even 3×3 sheet, one full character per cell, no overlaps, labels, numbers
or grid lines. Same anchor/baseline and equal padding in every cell. Reading
left-to-right, top-to-bottom: run-contact, run-pass, run-push, jump-rise,
jump-apex, jump-fall, landing-compress, hit-stagger, finish-awakened. Use clear
but modest anticipation, squash-and-stretch and secondary overlap. Perfectly
flat `#00FFFF` across the sheet; no shadow, gradient, floor, halo, reflection,
text, watermark, extra objects or effects outside the silhouette.

### Devi motion direction

Impulse and courage. Bold forward-leading elasticity; enormous mouth and long
horns carry follow-through. Run begins squashed, passes through neutral and
releases upward/forward. Rise stretches; apex rounds; fall widens; landing
compresses; hit recoils asymmetrically; finish lifts proudly with contained
chartreuse glow. Never add a giant eye, legs or Carlitos anatomy.

### Lovu motion direction

Listening and focus. Economical, grounded, exact rhythm; side arms behave like
a quiet metronome. Small run amplitude, controlled vertical rise, squared
apex, compact fall, precise landing and short annoyed recoil. Finish opens the
posture but never changes the flat mouth into a smile. No new limbs or Carlitos
anatomy.

### Divu motion direction

Joy and rebound. Buoyant circular rhythm like a soft musical ball. The run
squashes, rolls subtly and springs; rise stretches, apex returns to a circle,
fall widens, landing gives the strongest soft squash, hit wobbles safely and
finish hovers proudly. Keep the simple happy face; never add arms, legs,
aggression or Carlitos anatomy.
